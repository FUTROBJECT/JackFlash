Frequently asked

How do I unlock a module I bought on another device?

Open the Parent Zone (tap the gear, then answer the multiplication question), go to Modules, and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID or Google account, so they restore for free on any device signed in to the same account.

Is my child's data private?

Yes. JackFlash collects no personal information and stores all practice data only on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I set up more than one child?

On the home screen, tap Add Player. Each child gets their own profile, avatar, progress, and streak. Everyone shares one device and one app.

What's free, and what costs money?

The entire Multiply & Divide module is free. The Fractions module lets you try its Foundations group free; unlocking the rest is a one-time purchase. There are no subscriptions and no ads.

How does the app decide what to practice?

JackFlash uses Smart Practice — an adaptive engine that shows your child more of the facts they're still learning and fewer they've already mastered, with spaced review so facts stick for good.

I bought a module but don't see it.

Try Restore Purchases in the Parent Zone Modules screen. If it still doesn't appear, email adamlaserlab@gmail.com with the date and email address used for the purchase and we'll sort it out.
