✓ Is this really Singapore Math?

Yes — the CPA progression (concrete → pictorial → abstract), bar models, and number bonds, with scaffolds that fade as your child gets stronger.

✓ What does “mastered” mean?

Fast and unaided: three correct answers with no scaffold on screen. Then the fact moves to long-term review so it stays mastered.

✓ Is Smart Practice AI?

No. It’s rule-based: five categories and spaced repetition on a widening schedule. You can see exactly why each fact appears.

✓ What data do you collect?

None. Everything lives on your device — no accounts, no network, no ads. There’s nothing to leak because nothing is collected.

✓ How many kids can use it?

Multiple kids on one device, each with their own profile and progress.
