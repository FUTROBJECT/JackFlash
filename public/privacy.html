JackFlash Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 July 2026

The short version: JackFlash does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you or your child. Everything your child does stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

Who we are

JackFlash is a math-practice app made by Laser Lab Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information. If anything here is unclear, email us at adamlaserlab@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. JackFlash does not require an account and does not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location, or any other personal information to function.

The practice data the app creates — profiles you set up, which facts your child has practiced, progress, streaks, and achievements — is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

Children's privacy

JackFlash is designed for children and is safe under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. Because the app collects no personal information and sends nothing off the device, there is no children's data for us to access, share, or sell.

In-app purchases

JackFlash offers optional one-time purchases to unlock additional practice modules. When you make a purchase:

  • The payment itself is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see or receive your card number or billing details.
  • We use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, to verify and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated identifier for the app installation and non-personal transaction details (for example, which product was bought). It does not receive your child's name or practice data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

If you never make a purchase, no purchase-related information is processed at all.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

There are none. JackFlash contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not use any device advertising identifier.

Data retention and deletion

Because all data lives on your device, you are always in control of it. Deleting a profile in the app removes its data. Uninstalling the app removes all JackFlash data from the device.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and revise the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email adamlaserlab@gmail.com.
